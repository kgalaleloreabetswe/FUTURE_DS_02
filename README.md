# FUTURE_DS_02
This is a real-world customer retention and churn analytics project focused on analysing customer behaviour, identifying churn drivers, and generating actionable business insights to improve customer retention and reduce revenue loss. The project simulates the work of a business/data analyst within a SaaS/subscription-based business environment.


The analysis was conducted using the Telco Customer Churn dataset and focuses on understanding:
Why customers leave the platform, 
Which customer segments are most likely to churn, 
Customer lifetime and retention patterns, 
Business strategies to improve retention and recurring revenue, 


**Tools & Technologies Used:** 


**Microsoft Excel** – initial data exploration, validation, and preprocessing, 
**Power BI** – dashboard development, KPI reporting, and data visualisation
**Power Query (Power BI)** – data cleaning, transformation, grouping, and ETL processes
**DAX** – calculated measures, churn metrics, retention KPIs, and revenue analysis


**Project Objectives**


Analyse customer churn and retention trends, 
Identify high-risk customer segments, 
Evaluate customer lifetime patterns using tenure analysis, 
Analyse the impact of contracts, services, pricing, and payment methods on churn, 
Generate business-focused recommendations to improve customer retention, 
Data Processing. 

The dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query in Power BI, including:
Removing inconsistencies and formatting issues, 
Grouping customer tenure into lifecycle categories, 
Preparing structured tables for analysis and visualisation, 
Creating calculated fields and measures for churn analysis. 


Key KPIs and business measures were created using DAX, including:
Total Customers, Active Customers,Churned Customers, Churn Rate, Retention Rate, Revenue at Risk, Average Monthly Charges, Average Customer Tenure, Average Revenue per Churned Customer.


**Key Insights:** 


Overall churn rate: 26.54%
Retention rate: 73.46%
Average customer tenure: 32.37 months
Churned customers had a lower average tenure of 17.98 months
Churn Drivers:
Month-to-month contracts had the highest churn rate (42.71%), 
Two-year contracts showed the strongest retention (2.83% churn). 
Fiber optic customers had significantly higher churn (41.89%) compared to DSL and non-internet users. 
Customers using electronic checks had the highest churn rate (45.29%). 
Automatic payment methods showed lower churn levels. 
Customers paying higher monthly charges were more likely to churn. 
Highest churn occurred within the R80–100 monthly charge range. 


**High-Risk Customer Profile**


The highest-risk customer segment consisted of:
Month-to-month contract customers,
Fiber optic internet users,
Electronic check payment users and
Customers without technical support services. 


**Strategic Recommendations**


Encourage customers to move toward long-term contracts using incentives and loyalty rewards. 
Improve onboarding and engagement during the first 6–12 months of the customer lifecycle. 
Promote automatic payment methods to improve customer commitment. 
Investigate pricing and service concerns among fiber optic customers. 
Expand technical support offerings to improve customer satisfaction and retention. 


**Key Outcome:**


This project demonstrates end-to-end business intelligence and analytics skills, including:
Data cleaning and transformation,
Dashboard development,
DAX calculations and KPI creation,
Customer churn and retention analysis,
Business storytelling and insight generation,
Data-driven decision making.

The project highlights how data analytics can support customer retention strategies, reduce churn risk, and improve long-term recurring revenue growth.
